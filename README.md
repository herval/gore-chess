# GORE CHESS

Battle-Chess-style pixel-art chess where every capture is a fight to the death. Open `index.html` in a browser (no build, no server).

- Every piece is an animated, poseable rig built from hand-authored pixel parts, and each type kills differently:
  - Pawn: skewers with a spear
  - Knight: cleaves the victim in half, entrails and all
  - Bishop: explodes the skull
  - Rook golem: pulverizes the victim into a pancake
  - Queen: lightning-gibs or incinerates the victim to bones
  - King: takes the arm off, then the head
- Blood, pools, gibs, entrails and corpses bake permanently into the board. Killers get bloodier, drip from their weapons and leave footprints. Flies gather over the dead.
- The camera shakes, zooms, hit-stops and slows down during kills, and blood splatters the lens.
- Full chess rules. The alpha-beta AI runs in a Web Worker at 3 difficulties, plus a bloodthirsty AI-vs-AI "carnage" mode.
- Checkmate ends with an on-screen execution of the king.

Controls: mouse · SPACE hasten · M music · N sound · ESC menu
